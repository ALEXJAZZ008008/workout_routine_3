\textbf{WORKOUT INSTRUCTIONS \& PROGRESSION}\par\vspace{1.0mm}
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.485\linewidth}\raggedright
\textbf{Warm-up:} W = 6 reps at 40\%, 4 at 60\%, 2 at 80\% of working load (rounded to available weights). Complete all 3 sets. Only in the session(s) marked beside W.\par\vspace{0.8mm}
\textbf{Partners:} In slots 8 and 9, Alex and Kathi train simultaneously.\par\vspace{0.8mm}
\textbf{Loads:} DB loads kg/hand unless single. Machines use selected weight.\par\vspace{0.8mm}
\textbf{Schedule:} A/B/C in order, 2 sets per assigned exercise. Perform movements in numerical order.\par\vspace{0.8mm}
\textbf{Reps:} Each printed target applies to every working set. Advance to the next printed target after all sets reach the current target; otherwise repeat.
\end{minipage}\hfill
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.485\linewidth}\raggedright
\textbf{Weight:} Increase after all sets reach the maximum; restart at the minimum. If the new weight is too heavy, keep trying until you reach the target repetitions.\par\vspace{0.8mm}
\textbf{Effort:} 1 RIR. Air bike: all-out sprint.\par\vspace{0.8mm}
\textbf{Rest:} Alternate sets with your partner; allow extra recovery as needed. Recover between air-bike intervals.\par\vspace{0.8mm}
\textbf{Plateaus:} Technique, control and ROM gains count. If half or more movements stall or regress for 3 consecutive sessions, skip the next session, then resume.
\end{minipage}
